Designed and developed an interactive Sales Dashboard in Microsoft Excel using a multi-country financial dataset. Built dynamic Pivot Tables and Charts to analyze sales, profit, discount bands, and product performance across 5 countries and 6 business segments. Implemented slicers for real-time filtering by Country, Date, Segment, and Month. Uncovered key business insights including identification of the top-performing Government segment (~67% of total profit) and underperforming Enterprise segment (operating at a loss of ~$614K).

Cleaned and structured raw financial data across 700+ records for dashboard-ready analysis
Created 5+ interactive charts: bar, pie, donut, and line charts across multiple analysis sheets
Derived profit margin insights showing near-equal ~20% distribution across all 5 countries
Identified Paseo as the top product ($33M sales) and Enterprise as the loss-making segment
